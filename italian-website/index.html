Intro




Hello! My name is Nathan, and I created this page to share my ratings of four Italian dishes. I chose Italian food because of its popularity and variety of different dishes, with each dish offering something unique while sharing many of the same ingredients. I thought it was interesting that you can expand into so many unique dishes using a lot of the same ingredients, so I selected four dishes that share those same ingredients. I'm not an expert in rating food, but it would be fun to do!

How will it be rated?




There are four dishes I have picked out to rate, and each dish will receive a score and an explanation of why that dish received that score. I will give my honest opinion of each dish, with each dish being rated 1 through 9. A good score for a dish is a 7 or above, with 5 and 6 being average, and anything 4 or below is not good. I will focus on 3 things per dish: Taste, Texture, and Smell. Each category is rated 1-3, which is added up to create the 1-9 overall rating.

Just an opinion




All this is my opinion and far from factual, so don’t take any of my ratings to heart. So go ahead and start reading my reviews, click on the links above to move from dish to dish.
